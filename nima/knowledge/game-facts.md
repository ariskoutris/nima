# Game facts

- The D-pad moves you, or the cursor in a menu. A talks to what you face, confirms and advances
  text; in a dungeon it attacks. B opens the menu or cancels. SELECT shows the dungeon map.
- Steps are 16 pixels, so 8 pixels off to the side is often as close as you can line up.
- To talk to a character, walk toward it until a step doesn't move you, then press A instead of
  stepping around it; one step away is not yet in reach. Facing up or down, it may be up to 8
  pixels to the side, so a diagonal counts; facing left or right, it must be at most 4 pixels
  above or below. A shopkeeper is talked to across the counter, even when it stands a step to
  the side.
- Talking opens a dialogue box; no box means you haven't talked. After the last box closes the
  conversation is over, and A again restarts it.
- Walking off an area's edge fades to black and loads the next area; you are there once the
  screen is back and your position has jumped.
